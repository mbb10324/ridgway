# Code of Conduct

**Updated By: Miles Breman**

**Updated On: 9/4/2024**

## Introduction

We have adapted the standard code of conduct and require the following behavior for contributors when interacting with each other:

### Be Committed

Support your team and organization. Act with integrity and dedication in all tasks and interactions.

### Fulfill Responsibilities

Complete your tasks to the best of your ability. Be reliable and accountable in all professional and personal endeavors.

### Honor Others

Treat everyone with dignity and consideration. Value diverse perspectives and foster a positive, inclusive environment.

### Put Others First

Act in the best interest of your team and community. Offer help willingly and work to achieve common goals over personal gain.

### Maintain Excellence

Uphold high ethical standards and professionalism. Act in a manner that reflects positively on yourself and the organization.

### Be Honest and Transparent

Communicate openly and act with honesty. Ensure your actions and decisions are consistent with ethical standards.

### Face Challenges Bravely

Stand firm in your convictions, even when facing adversity. Take responsible risks to achieve goals and support others.
