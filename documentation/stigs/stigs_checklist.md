## Overview

If you're unfamiliar with STIGs or their role in application development, please refer to `stigs_explained.md` within this folder. This file will explain what the checklist is for and how to use it to ensure your application meets Army security requirements.

## Getting Started

To view the checklist, you can use the 'STIG Viewer,' which can be downloaded from the [DoD Cyber Exchange STIG Tools Page](https://public.cyber.mil/stigs/srg-stig-tools/).

The checklist relevant to this boilerplate application, as well as most web applications developed to meet Army security standards, is the `Application Security and Development STIG`. You can find it by filtering through the [STIGs Document Library](https://public.cyber.mil/stigs/downloads/).

_Disclaimer: STIGs are regularly updated. Please check the document library frequently to ensure you have the most up-to-date STIG. Also, visit the DoD Cyber Exchange web page or consult cybersecurity authorities to ensure you are using the proper STIG/STIGs for your project._

### Using the STIG Viewer

#### Starting with the Provided Checklist:

1. Launch the STIG Viewer application.
2. Select `Open` next to the Checklists option.
3. Locate the `.cklb` file included in this directory.
4. Your checklist should now be ready. Use the 1, 2, 3, and 4 keys on your keyboard to change each STIG's determination.

#### Starting with a New Checklist from the DoD Exchange:

1. Launch the STIG Viewer application.
2. Click `Open` next to the STIG Viewer option.
3. Navigate to the `Application Security and Development` folder, locate the `.xml` file, and open it.
4. Add your STIG to your local STIG Library.
5. Once your STIG is included in your library, locate the `Open Checklists` icon in the top right of the application and click on it.
6. Select `Application Security and Development` from your STIG Library on the left and press the plus icon.
7. The `Application Security and Development STIG` should now appear in your checklist. In the top right, click the `Fill Checklist` button.
8. Your checklist should now be populated. Use the 1, 2, 3, and 4 keys on your keyboard to change each STIG's determination.

### Saving the Checklist

To maintain a history of the file, replace the date in the name when saving the checklist. For instance, if today's date is December 12, 2024, name the checklist `STIG_Checklist_12DEC24.cklb`.

By following these steps, you'll be able to efficiently use and maintain your STIG checklist, ensuring your application complies with Army security standards.
